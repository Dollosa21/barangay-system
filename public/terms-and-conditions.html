Authorized use

This workspace is for authorized barangay administrators and staff performing official beneficiary assistance, livelihood, training, attendance, reporting, and related administrative duties.

Accurate records

Enter information carefully, use the applicant's supporting documents, and avoid duplicate registrations. Review a record before saving it. A reference number and registration date and time are created for each submission.

Program decisions

A registration is an application record, not a promise of approval, release, funding, employment, or participation. Approval and release decisions must follow the barangay's eligibility rules, available resources, review process, and applicable government policies.

Administrator responsibilities

  • Use only your own account and keep your password private.
  • Keep resident information confidential and share it only for authorized program work.
  • Correct errors promptly and preserve a clear record of official changes.
  • Do not use the system for harassment, discrimination, fraud, personal sales, or unauthorized data collection.
  • Report suspected unauthorized access, loss of records, or incorrect releases to the barangay office.

System limitations

This local development installation stores application and attendance records in its configured MySQL database. Access depends on the server and database remaining available and on maintaining secure, current backups. It is not a substitute for an approved production records system, official registry, legal advice, or a government decision process.

Updates and support

Barangay management may update forms, program rules, workflows, and these terms as services change. Questions about eligibility, assistance, livelihood support, or corrections should be directed to the barangay office.
