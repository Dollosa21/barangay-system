Purpose of this system

The Barangay Beneficiary and Livelihood Program Management System helps authorized barangay staff register residents, assess assistance requests, coordinate livelihood support, schedule activities, and maintain program records.

Information collected

Records may include a resident's name, birth date, sex, contact details, address, household size, income information, assistance request, submitted requirements, livelihood project details, attendance, status, and registration date and time. Administrator account information is stored for workspace access.

How information is used

  • To review and process beneficiary assistance applications.
  • To match livelihood participants with training, tools, capital, mentoring, or market support.
  • To coordinate releases, activities, attendance, reports, and follow-up services.
  • To keep an accountable record of actions taken by the barangay program team.

Access and protection

Record endpoints require an authenticated administrator session. This local development installation stores records in its configured MySQL database, and encrypts application details using Laravel's application key. Keep the server, database credentials, and encryption key private. Do not export or share records without an official purpose and authority. A production deployment should use HTTPS, encrypted backups, and any security controls required by the barangay and applicable law.

Questions and corrections

For corrections, access requests, or concerns about a record, contact the barangay office or the designated data protection officer. Include the reference number when requesting a record review.
